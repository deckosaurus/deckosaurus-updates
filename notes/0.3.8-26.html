Deckosaurus 0.3.8

  • Transcripts keep their layout on Macs with always-visible scroll bars. With a mouse (no trackpad), or "Show scroll bars: Always", the transcript's scroll bar used to appear and disappear as a reply streamed in, and each time it did, tables and the turn's timestamp were laid out for a width the pane no longer had. The scroll bar's space is now kept, so the text no longer shifts. Trackpad Macs look exactly as before.
